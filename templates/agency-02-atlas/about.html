About

Why Atlas

Price per m² on every listing. Yield where we have the data.

Most agencies sell the view. We put the numbers next to it: what the building sold for last year, what similar units rent for, what the service charge is. Buyers decide faster and regret less. Sellers get offers from people who have already done the maths.

How we work

Four rules.

  1. 01

    Facts before adjectives.

    Price, m², yield, service charge, completion date. The adjectives can wait for the viewing.

  2. 02

    One person, start to end.

    The agent who shows you the property also runs the offer, the lawyer and the bank. Nobody is handed over.

  3. 03

    We say no to listings.

    No permit, unclear title, unrealistic price: we decline. Around a third of what developers send us never goes live.

  4. 04

    Reply within one working day.

    Every enquiry, every language, every channel. If we miss it, say so and we will fix it.
