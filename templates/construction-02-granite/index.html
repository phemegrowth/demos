How we build

Six steps, no surprises.

You see the price before we start and the progress every week. Here is how a project runs with us.

  1. Meet

    We visit the plot or property, listen to what you need and tell you what is realistic. No charge.

    Week 1

  2. Design and permit

    Your architect or ours. We check the drawings for buildability and file the permit.

    2 to 6 months

  3. Quote

    An itemised fixed price with a payment schedule tied to milestones, not dates on a calendar.

    2 weeks

  4. Build

    Our crews, our machines, one site manager. A written progress report every Friday with photos.

    6 to 18 months

  5. Inspect

    Walk-through with you and the engineer. A snag list we close before we ask for the final payment.

    2 weeks

  6. Hand over

Questions we get

The four things everyone asks first.

If yours is not here, call. It is faster than email.

How much does it cost per square metre?

For a house in Cyprus, a realistic range is 1,400 to 2,200 EUR per m2 plus VAT for the build, depending on the plot, the structure and the finishes. Apartment blocks come in lower per m2; renovations vary most. We give a fixed itemised price after the site visit and the drawings, not a number on the phone.

How long will it take?

A house takes 10 to 14 months on site once the permit is in. A block of 18 units takes about 18 months. A full renovation takes 3 to 5 months. The permit itself is the part nobody controls: 2 to 6 months is typical. We put the dates in the contract and report against them every week.

Do you handle the permits?

Yes. Our engineer files the planning and building permits with your architect, follows them up and handles the final certificate of approval. If you already have a permit, we check it against the drawings before we price anything.

Who manages the site day to day?

One named site manager, on your site, from the first dig to the handover. You get their mobile number. Andreas or Michalis visits every site at least once a week, and you receive a written report with photos every Friday.
