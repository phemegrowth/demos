Case study, 2024

A 1980s house in Aradippou, rebuilt around the family living in it.

Full renovation of a 180 m2 two-storey house: new roof, new services, new kitchen and bathrooms, open-plan ground floor. Finished in 19 weeks, 1 week ahead of the contract date.

What happened

Keep the walls, change everything inside them.

The family wanted to stay in the house during the work, which set the order of everything. We did the roof and the first floor while they lived downstairs, then moved them up and opened the ground floor into one room with a steel beam where two walls had been.

The old plumbing was worse than the survey showed. We found it in week 3, priced two options the same afternoon and replaced every pipe in the house for 6,800 EUR more than the contract. That was the only variation.

Handover came with a folder: electrical certificate, plumbing test, roof warranty, appliance manuals and the as-built drawings.
