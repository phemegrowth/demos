A family firm that still carries its own tools.

Started in 1999 by two brothers with one mixer and a borrowed truck. Now 46 people, our own yard in Larnaca and work across Cyprus and Greece. Still two brothers at the top.

The story

Our father laid stone. Andreas and Michalis started with renovations in 1999 because that is what people near us needed.

The first house came in 2003, for a neighbour who had watched us work on three kitchens and decided we could be trusted with the rest. The first apartment block came in 2009, for a developer who had tried two other contractors on the same plot.

We grew slowly and on purpose. Every crew is led by someone who has been with us at least 5 years. Our machines are ours. Our site managers are employees, not subcontractors, and they stay on one project until it is handed over.

Christina, Andreas's daughter, joined in 2019 and now runs our developer projects. The next generation is already on site.

How we work

Six rules we do not bend.

They cost us the occasional job. They have never cost us a client.

  • 01

    One price, in writing, before we start.

    Itemised. If something changes, you get a written variation with a price before the work is done, not after.

  • 02

    One site manager, start to finish.

    You get their name and mobile number on day one. They do not move to another project halfway through yours.

  • 03

    A report every Friday.

    What was done, what is next, photos, and anything that needs your decision. Short, so you read it.

  • 04

    We say when it is not for us.

    If the job needs a specialist we are not, or the budget cannot build what you want, we tell you at the first meeting.

  • 05

    The snag list is ours to close.

    We walk the finished site with you and the engineer. We do not ask for the last payment until the list is empty.

  • 06

    Neighbours are told first.

    Before the first truck arrives, we knock on the doors on the street. Fewer complaints, faster permits, calmer sites.
