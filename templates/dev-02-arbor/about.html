About

We build fewer homes than most developers, and finish all of them.

Arbor Homes started in Limassol in 2014 with four houses and one rule: build each one as if we were going to live in it. 212 homes later, in Cyprus and Greece, the rule has not changed.

01

Small by choice

3 to 12 homes per project. Small enough that we know every buyer by name and every plot by its problems. We would rather do six projects well than one tower badly.

02

A+ is the minimum

Every home we have built since 2019 has an A+ energy certificate, issued by an independent assessor before handover. We will not sell a class B home, even if it is cheaper to build.

03

Dates in the contract

Completion dates are written in the contract with a penalty if we miss them. We have paid it once, in 2021. We would rather tell you the real date than a nice one.

How we build

From plot to keys in 20 months.

The same six steps on every project. You see each one as it happens.

  1. 01

    Plot and study

    We buy the plot ourselves, never on option. Soil study, sun study and a first energy model before any drawing.

    Month 0
  2. 02

    Design

    Our in-house architect draws the houses around the sun and the slope. Buyers who reserve early can change internal walls and finishes.

    Months 1 to 4
  3. 03

    Permits

    Planning and building permits are in hand before we sell. The permit number is on every brochure.

    Months 4 to 8
  4. 04

    Structure

    Reinforced concrete frame, then external insulation and triple glazing. An independent engineer checks every pour.

    Months 8 to 14
  5. 05

    Finishes and systems

    Heat pump, underfloor system, solar, charger. Tiles, kitchens and bathrooms from the agreed selection or your own.

    Months 14 to 19
  6. 06

    Certificate and keys

    Energy assessor visits, A+ certificate issued, snagging walk with you, then keys. One year of after-care follows.

    Month 20
