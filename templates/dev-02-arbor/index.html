Why A+ matters

The class on the certificate is money in your pocket.

Most homes in Cyprus and Greece are built to class B or worse. Here is what A+ changes, in the four places you feel it.

  1. 01

    Energy bill

    A+ means the house needs very little energy to stay comfortable. Our owners report bills far below what they paid in older flats of the same size.

    62%lower average yearly energy cost
  2. 02

    Insulation

    External wall insulation, insulated roof slab and triple glazing. The sun hits the outside of the wall, not your living room. It also keeps the street out.

    12 cmexternal insulation, 3 panes of glass
  3. 03

    Solar

    Photovoltaic panels on every roof, sized for the home, wired to the grid under net metering. Hot water comes from the sun too.

    5 kWpstandard per house, more on request
  4. 04

    EV-ready

    A 7 kW charging point in every garage or parking space, on its own circuit. Charge at home overnight from your own solar credit.

    7 kWcharger at every parking space
