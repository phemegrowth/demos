Eight houses, no two facing each other.

The plots sit on a slope, so every house looks over the one below and out to the sea. Living rooms open on the south side, bedrooms on the cooler north. Each house has a 7 by 3.5 metre pool, covered parking with an EV charger and a storage room.

Construction is reinforced concrete with 12 cm external insulation, triple-glazed aluminium windows and a 5 kWp solar array. Underfloor heating and cooling run on a heat pump. The A+ certificate is issued before handover, not promised after.
