AboutEst. 2012

We draw the building first. Then we sell it. Never the other way round.

The firm

Architects who build.

LINEA started in 2012 as an architecture studio in Limassol that was tired of watching good drawings get cheapened on site.

So we became the developer. We buy the plot, draw the building, run the site and sell the apartments. One company, one signature on every decision, from the first line to the keys.

Since then: 6 buildings, 612 apartments, 84,200 m², 0 late handovers. Limassol first, Glyfada and Voula since 2022.

We are small on purpose. Two projects on site at a time, never more. That is how a 2.9 m ceiling stays 2.9 m.

Principles

Five rules.

  1. The date in the contract is the date.

    Every handover since 2012 has been on or before the contract date. We write a penalty clause in our own contract because we expect to never pay it.

  2. The drawing is not a suggestion.

    The architect who drew the building is on site every week. Substitutions need her signature, and she rarely gives it.

  3. Fewer units, bigger rooms.

    We build 15 to 20% fewer apartments than the plot allows. Buyers pay for space they can feel, not for a number on a brochure.

  4. Price once.

    Launch prices are published and hold for 30 days. No secret lists, no "price on request", no discounts for friends.

  5. Show the site, not the render.

    Monthly site photos go to every buyer. The render sells the idea; the photo proves the progress.
