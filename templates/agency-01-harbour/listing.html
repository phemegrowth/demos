The home

Built in 2019 on a quiet street behind the Glyfada marina.

The ground floor is one open space: kitchen, dining and sitting room, with sliding glass along the south side onto the pool terrace. The kitchen has a marble island, a wine fridge and a separate utility room.

Four bedrooms are upstairs, each with its own bathroom or shower room and access to a long balcony with a sea view over the rooftops. The fifth bedroom is a self-contained guest suite at garden level with its own entrance.

Outside: a 12 m curved pool, a covered dining area with a built-in barbecue, mature olive trees and bougainvillea, and a double garage with storage. Energy class B+. Freehold.
