Sell with us

What happens when you list a home with us.

  1. A valuation in writing.

    We compare recent sales on your street, not asking prices, and give you the number and the reasoning on paper.

  2. Photography, video and a floor plan.

    A professional shoot, drone footage where it helps, and a measured plan. Ready within 7 days of instruction.

  3. Buyers we already know.

    Your home goes first to our registered buyers, then to the portals and our own channels in 4 languages.

  4. One agent for every viewing.

    The same person meets each buyer, reports back the same day and negotiates on your behalf.

  5. Through to completion.

    We work with your lawyer on the contract, the deposit and the transfer so nothing stalls.
