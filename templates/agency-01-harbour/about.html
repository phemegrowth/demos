About the agency

An agency built on repeat clients.

Most of the homes we sell come to us by recommendation. That only happens when the last sale went well.

Our story

Started in Limassol in 1998 with two desks and a telephone.

Elena Charalambous opened the first office on Harbour Street to sell the homes her father had built. The rule from day one was simple: tell the client the true number, even when it loses the instruction.

Lettings came in 2006, the Larnaca and Nicosia desk in 2012, and in 2021 we opened in Glyfada to serve the Cyprus clients who were buying in Greece and the Greek families moving the other way.

Today we are 4 agents and 3 support staff across 2 offices. Small on purpose: every client knows exactly who is handling their home.

How we work

Four rules we do not bend.

  1. The true number, in writing.

    A valuation is based on what sold, not what is listed. We put it on paper with the comparables so you can check it.

  2. One agent, start to finish.

    You are not passed between a valuer, a viewing assistant and a negotiator. The person who valued your home sells it.

  3. Both sides get the facts.

    A buyer hears about the damp patch before the offer, not after. It keeps sales together and it keeps our name clean.

  4. No pressure, no deadlines invented.

    There is never another buyer about to sign unless there really is. Take the time you need to decide.
