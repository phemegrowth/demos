Services

Four disciplines. One contract.

You sign once. The architect who draws your house manages the crew that pours it. No handover between firms, no finger pointing, no drawing that cannot be built.

01

Architecture.

Site study and feasibility before you buy the plot.

Concept, planning permit, building permit. Detail drawings to 1:5 where the concrete meets the glass.

  • 01.1Feasibility and massing
  • 01.2Planning and building permits
  • 01.3Detail and shop drawings
  • 01.4Site supervision

02

Engineering.

Structural and civil engineering in house. Exposed concrete needs the engineer and the architect at the same desk.

Seismic design to Eurocode 8 for Cyprus and Greece. Energy study and MEP coordination.

  • 02.1Structural design, in-situ and precast
  • 02.2Seismic and foundation design
  • 02.3Energy performance study
  • 02.4MEP coordination

03

Construction.

Our own teams for formwork, reinforcement and pouring. Specialist subcontractors we have worked with for years for the rest.

Fixed price. Fixed programme. A site manager who reports to you every week with photos and the plan for the next 7 days.

  • 03.1Earthworks and foundations
  • 03.2Exposed concrete frame
  • 03.3Envelope: steel, glass, timber
  • 03.4Handover and 10-year structural guarantee

04

Interiors.

Concrete carries into the rooms. We draw the kitchen, the joinery, the lighting and the stone so the inside is one material language with the outside.

Furniture and loose items on request, sourced from workshops in Cyprus and Greece.

  • 04.1Joinery and kitchens
  • 04.2Lighting design
  • 04.3Stone and floor schedules
  • 04.4Furniture sourcing

How we price

Design fees as a percentage of construction cost. Construction at a fixed price after permit drawings. Houses from 2,400 EUR per m2 plus VAT, shell and core; a full estimate follows the first meeting.
