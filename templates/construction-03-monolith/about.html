Studio

MONOLITH started in Limassol in 2014 as two architects and a site foreman who were tired of watching good drawings get value-engineered by someone else. The answer was to pour the concrete ourselves.

Today the studio is 26 people: architects, structural engineers, a quantity surveyor, site managers and a formwork crew that has been with us since the first house. In 2023 we opened a second office in Glyfada to serve work along the coast of Greece.

We take on 4 to 6 projects a year. Houses, small apartment buildings, offices. Enough to give each one a partner on site every week.
