Project 01 / Residence / Built 2024

Tala House.

Client
Private
Place
Tala, Paphos, Cyprus
Programme
Single family house, 4 bedrooms
Area
412 m2 covered, 2,100 m2 plot
Year
Design 2022, built 2023 to 2024
Structure
In-situ concrete, 24 m clear span roof

A single white concrete bar on a slope above Tala, facing south to the sea. One long room along the view, bedrooms behind it, the roof carried on two walls so the glass line stays uninterrupted for 24 m.

Design and construction were one contract. The formwork was drawn by the same engineer who checked it on site, which is why the board marks line up at every corner.
