Process

Five steps. One contractor.

No handovers between a fabricator, a galvaniser and an erector. We do all of it, so the date we give you holds.

  1. Measure

    We survey on site and check every dimension against the drawings.

  2. Design

    Shop drawings and connection details, signed off by your engineer.

  3. Fabricate

    Cut, drilled, welded and checked in the workshop.

  4. Galvanise

    Hot-dip galvanised or powder coated, with certificates.

  5. Install

    Our own crews, our own cranes, on a fixed date.
