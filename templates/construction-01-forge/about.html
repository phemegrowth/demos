About

A workshop, not a middleman.

FORGE is a fabricator with its own hall, machines and crews. Nothing we sell is sub-contracted to another shop.

Workshop

2,400 m² under one roof.

The hall in Aradippou has two 10 t overhead cranes, a covered loading bay and a paint line. Steel arrives as plate and section, leaves as a numbered kit of parts ready to bolt together on site.

Every piece is marked with the job number and position. The erection crew works from the same drawing the welder used.

Machines

What the hall runs on.

  • CNC plasma table3 x 1.5 m, plate to 25 mm
  • Band sawSections to HEB 600
  • Press brake160 t, 3 m bed
  • Hydraulic shear3 m, plate to 12 mm
  • Welding bays8
  • Overhead cranes2 x 10 t
  • Mobile cranes35 t and 100 t, own fleet

Timeline

14 years, one address.

  1. 2012

    Two welders and a rented bay. Railings and gates for Larnaca homes.

  2. 2015

    First portal frame hall. Move to the current 2,400 m² workshop.

  3. 2018

    EN 1090 certification. CNC plasma and press brake installed.

  4. 2022

    Own crane fleet. Second erection crew. First jobs in Greece.

  5. 2026

    Standard product range launched with fixed prices.
