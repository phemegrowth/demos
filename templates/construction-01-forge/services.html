Services

Four services. One workshop.

Everything is drawn, cut, welded and coated under one roof in Larnaca, then installed by our own crews. That is why our dates hold.

01

Structural steel

Structural steel.

Primary frames for industrial, commercial and residential buildings. We work from your engineer's design, produce the shop drawings, fabricate to EN 1090 EXC2 and erect with our own cranes.

  • Portal frame halls up to 40 m span
  • Mezzanine floors and platforms
  • Roof and floor beams for concrete buildings
  • Steel cores, lift shafts and stair towers
  • Connections, base plates and anchors
  • Erection with own crews and cranes

02

Metal frames

Metal frames.

Light steel framing for extensions, roof conversions, canopies and carports. Faster than concrete, lighter on the existing structure, and installed in days.

  • Roof extensions on existing buildings
  • Canopies and carports
  • Light steel frames for extensions
  • Solar panel support frames
  • Signage and billboard frames
  • Temporary works and shoring
Get a quote
03

Architectural metal

Architectural metal.

The metalwork people see and touch. Gates, railings, staircases and pergolas, made to measure from your drawings or our standard designs, finished in powder coat or galvanised.

  • Balcony and stair railings
  • Sliding and swing gates, motor ready
  • Internal and external staircases
  • Pergolas and shade structures
  • Window grilles and security doors
  • Brise soleil and cladding frames
Get a quote
04

Fabrication

Fabrication to drawing.

Send the DXF or PDF, get the parts. Cutting, bending, drilling and welding on CNC machines, with a full inspection record. For contractors, engineers and other workshops.

  • Plasma and band saw cutting to 20 mm
  • Press brake bending to 3 m
  • MIG, TIG and arc welding by certified welders
  • Drilling and tapping of plates and sections
  • Brackets, plates and assemblies in batches
  • Galvanising and powder coating arranged
Get a quote

Installation

Our own crews on site.

We do not hand the frame to a third party to erect. The people who built it in the workshop bolt it in on site, with our cranes and our lifts. One foreman, one phone number, one date.

Site safety is managed to our own plan and your site rules. Risk assessments and method statements come with every job.
