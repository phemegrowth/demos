Structural steel

Aradippou logistics hall.

A 6,200 m² distribution hall with a 36 m clear span, built for a Larnaca logistics operator on a fixed programme of 14 weeks.

The brief

Clear span, no internal columns.

The client needed a hall that forklifts could cross without weaving around columns, with 10 m clear height for racking and loading docks along one side. The site was a cleared plot in the Aradippou industrial area, with foundations by the main contractor. The frame had to go up between the slab pour and the arrival of the cladding contractor: a 5 week window.

What we built

310 tonnes, 18 frames.

A hot-rolled portal frame of 18 bays at 6 m centres, IPE 600 rafters with haunches, HEB 400 columns, and a 36 m clear span. Roof and wall purlins in cold-formed Z sections, cross bracing in each end bay, and a mezzanine office block of 480 m² at the dock end.

Shop drawings were produced from the engineer's design in 3 weeks. Fabrication ran 6 weeks in parallel with the groundworks. All steel was hot-dip galvanised and the main frame painted in the client's blue. Erection took 4 weeks with two crews and a 100 t mobile crane.

The frame was up a week early and nothing on site had to wait for steel.

Project manager, main contractor
