A letter

A letter from Elena

I used to work for a large agency. We had 600 homes on the books and I could not have told you which one had the better morning light.

So I left, and I kept 8. Eight is a number I can know.

Every home here I have chosen, photographed on a day I picked, and priced with the owner at the kitchen table. When you call, you get me. When you visit, I bring the keys myself.

It is a slower way to do this. I think it is the only honest one.

Elena

Elena Markou, Limassol, October 2026
