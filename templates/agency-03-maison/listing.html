The home

You arrive from above. The road drops from the village towards the sea and the house appears below you, white, low, with the pool running along its front like a line drawn in green ink. By the time you park you have already seen the view, which is unfair to the house, because the house is good.

Inside, the plan is simple. One long room for living and eating, open on both sides, with the kitchen at the far end. Four bedrooms upstairs, each with its own bathroom and its own piece of the sea. The main bedroom has a terrace the width of the house.

A house that was built to be lived in through August, not photographed in May.Elena, after the second visit

The owners rebuilt the garden in 2025 with olives, carob and lavender, and put in a heated 14 metre pool. There is a studio in the garden with its own entrance, which has been an office, a guest suite and once, briefly, a painting room. Agios Tychonas village is 4 minutes up the road, the old Limassol coast road is 2 minutes down it, and the sea is a 10 minute walk.

It suits a family who wants a real house in Limassol without living in the middle of it. I have shown it 3 times. All 3 people are still thinking about it, which tells you something.
