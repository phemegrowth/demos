About

One agent. Eight homes.

I am Elena Markou. I sell a few very good homes in Cyprus and Greece, and I would rather sell one well than ten quickly.

The story

Limassol, 2026

I grew up in Limassol in a house my grandfather built, with a lemon tree in the courtyard and a view that stopped at the neighbour's wall. I did not know houses could be a job until I was 24 and someone offered me one.

For 11 years I worked for one of the largest agencies on the island. I was good at it. I sold a lot. By the end I had 600 homes on my list and a phone that never stopped, and I noticed that I had started to describe houses I had never been inside.

In 2021 I left and kept 8. I called it Maison Elena because a friend from Lyon said it sounded like a dress shop, and I liked that. A dress shop does not have 600 dresses. It has the ones that are worth your time.

Since then I have added Greece, because half my buyers asked for it and because I spend my summers there. I still answer my own phone. I still bring the keys.

How I work.

Three rules I do not break, for buyers and for owners.

I.

I visit before I list.

Every home in the collection I have walked through at least twice, at different hours. I photograph it on a day I choose. If I would not want to spend a week there, it does not go in.

II.

I say no, often.

I turn down more homes than I take on, and I tell buyers when a house is wrong for them. It costs me sales. It is why people send their friends.

III.

I stay after the keys.

For 12 months after a sale you can still call me about the plumber, the school, the tax adviser. Most of my buyers are from abroad. The sale is the easy part.

What I look for

Light, proportion, quiet.

Not the biggest house on the street. The one with the best morning, the right distance from the sea, and rooms you can furnish without an architect. I like houses that were built to be lived in through August.
