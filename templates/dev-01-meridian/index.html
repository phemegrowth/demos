The process

How buying off‑plan works.

Four steps from a drawing to a front door. Each one has a date, a document and a person you can call.

  1. 01

    Choose a home from the plans.

    You see the unit, the floor, the view and the finishes schedule. We hold it for 14 days against a €5,000 reservation, refunded in full if you change your mind.

  2. 02

    Sign the contract.

    Your lawyer reviews the sale agreement. On signing you pay 30 percent and the contract is filed at the Land Registry, so the home is legally yours from that day.

  3. 03

    Pay as we build.

    The balance follows the construction stages, not the calendar. Each payment is tied to a milestone you can visit, and each is covered by a bank guarantee.

  4. 04

    Receive the keys.

    Handover with a snag-free walkthrough, the energy certificate and the title deed application already filed. Our aftercare team stays with you for 24 months.
