About

Built slowly, sold honestly.

A developer that puts the delivery date in the contract and has met it on every project since 2009.

The founder

Still on site every Friday.

I started Meridian in 2009 with one plot in Limassol, a builder I trusted and a promise I was not sure I could keep: a date on paper, and the keys on that date.

Off-plan buying has a bad name in parts of the Mediterranean. People paid for drawings and waited years for a building that changed shape, or never came. I wanted to sell the opposite: a home you can see in full before the first pour, a price that does not move, and payments you only make when you can stand on the floor you are paying for.

Seventeen years later the company is 32 people across Limassol, Paphos and Glyfada. We still build with the same two contractors. We still keep our own site managers on every project. And I still walk every site on a Friday, because the buyers who trusted a drawing deserve someone who checks the concrete.

If you are thinking of buying with us, read the four steps on the home page, then call. You will speak to a person who knows the project, not a call centre.

Alexandros VarnavaFounder and managing director

Timeline

Seventeen years, seven projects.

  1. 2009

    Founded in Limassol.

    One plot on the hill above the old town, 8 apartments, all sold before the roof.

  2. 2012

    First villas, Larnaca.

    Eleven townhouses in Pervolia, delivered 3 weeks before the contract date.

  3. 2016

    Elia Court starts.

    Our largest project to that point, 30 apartments by the Larnaca marina.

  4. 2019

    Paphos office opens.

    A second team for the west of the island, led by our construction director.

  5. 2022

    Elia Court completed and sold out.

    Separate title deeds issued within 11 months of handover.

  6. 2023

    First project in Greece.

    Thalassa Residences in Voula, 22 apartments a short walk from the beach.

  7. 2025

    Thalassa delivered.

    Handed over in June 2025, on the date in the contract. Two penthouses remain.

  8. 2026

    Residence Kallithea on site.

    48 apartments in Limassol, 44 sold. Completion March 2028.
